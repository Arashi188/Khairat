A Letter For You

Happy birthday to my best girlrrr ❤️🥹🎂🫶🏽

But honestly, one thing that has been on my mind is how much I’m going to miss you now that you’ve graduated. 😭💔 The thought of not seeing you as often as I used to is actually getting to me because I’ve gotten so used to having you around. I know graduation is a beautiful thing, and I’m genuinely happy for you because you deserve every good thing coming your way. I’m proud of you, and I pray this is just the beginning of greater things in your life. But as happy as I am for you, a part of me is sad because I know things won’t be exactly the same anymore. We might not get to see each other as often, and we won’t have those random everyday moments like before, and honestly, I’m going to miss that so much. 🥹❤️

Happy birthday once again to my beautiful best girl. ❤️🎂🫂 Enjoy your day, allow yourself to be celebrated, and never forget how special you are to me. I might not get to see you as often anymore, but that doesn’t mean you’ll matter any less to me. If anything, this whole thing is making me realize even more how much I value having you in my life.

I’m going to miss you, girl, more than you can imagine. ❤️ I love you plenty, and I’m rooting for you always. Happy birthday, my girl. May this new age be your absolute best one yet. 🥹❤️🎉
